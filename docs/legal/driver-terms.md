## 3. Who can drive

To apply you must:

- be at least **18** years old **[ATTORNEY: confirm]** and legally allowed to work in South Africa;
- have a valid South African ID or passport with a valid permit **[ATTORNEY: which documents are acceptable]**;
- for a scooter, motorcycle, car or bakkie: hold a valid driver's licence for that vehicle class and have the vehicle's registration (licence disc) current and in your name or with the owner's permission **[ATTORNEY: whether written owner permission must be uploaded]**;
- for a bicycle: no licence is required;
- have a smartphone that can share its location while you are online;
- give us a South African bank account in your name for payouts.

You upload your ID, and for a motorised vehicle your licence and vehicle registration, in the app. A platform operations reviewer checks them and approves or declines your application, with a reason recorded. We may ask for a new upload when a document expires.

## 4. Going online and receiving offers

- You go **online** in the app when you want to work. While online, the app sends your approximate position every few minutes so that offers can be matched to drivers near the shop. You may go offline at any time.
- Offers go to approved drivers near the shop and are time-limited. You may accept or decline any offer. The first driver to accept gets the delivery.
- While you hold a delivery you cannot accept another one.
- Skhokho Labs may pause the network, limit sign-ups or stop dispatch at any time for operational or safety reasons.

## 5. Doing a delivery

1. Go to the shop. Tell the app when you arrive.
2. The shop gives you a **6-digit pickup code**. Enter it to confirm you have the order. Five wrong entries lock the code; the shop must issue a new one.
3. Deliver to the address shown, following the customer's instructions. The app shows the customer's name, address, instructions and the contact number for that delivery only, from the moment you accept until the delivery ends.
4. At the address, the customer gives you their **drop-off code**. Enter it to complete the delivery. Never ask for the code before the customer has the order; never accept a code from the shop.
5. If you cannot deliver (nobody answers, wrong address, customer refuses), mark the delivery **failed** with a reason and return the order to the shop. Confirm the return in the app.

You must handle food and goods safely, keep them sealed, not open or consume them, and not hand them to anyone but the customer or the person they name. Treat shops and customers with courtesy. Comply with the road traffic laws at all times.
