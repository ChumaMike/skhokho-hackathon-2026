## 2. Plain words we use

- **Business / shop** — a business that has registered on Skhokho and sells, serves or delivers to you. The business is a separate legal person from Skhokho Labs.
- **Storefront** — the business's public page on Skhokho, whether at a Skhokho address or at the business's own domain.
- **Rewards card / membership** — your relationship with one business, run on Skhokho, identified by a card code that only that business can see.
- **Skhokholetha** — the delivery network run by Skhokho Labs, where independent drivers deliver orders for businesses that choose to use it.
- **Order / booking** — a purchase or an appointment you make through a storefront.

## 3. Skhokho Labs is the platform, the business is the seller

Every order and booking you place is a contract **between you and the business**, not between you and Skhokho Labs. The business decides what it sells, at what price, when it is open, whether it delivers, and how it handles returns and complaints. Each storefront shows the business's own legal name, address, contact details, returns and cancellation terms and complaints route in its footer, as the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA) requires of online sellers.

Skhokho Labs provides the software: the storefront, the basket and checkout, bookings, the rewards card, order messages, and (where a business uses it) Skhokholetha delivery.

## 4. Your mobile number and your rewards card

You join a business's rewards programme by giving your mobile number (and, if you choose, your name). That business then sees a card code for you, your visits and your rewards at that business.

- A business sees only its own relationship with you. **We never show one business your activity at another business.**
- We will not link your rewards across different businesses unless you give a separate, clearly worded consent for that, and only once the Information Regulator has authorised that processing. That feature is switched off until then. (See the Privacy Notice.)
- The rewards and loyalty rules — how many visits earn what — are the business's own and may change; the business will tell you how.
- You can leave a business's programme at any time by asking the business or by contacting us (section 16).

Public forms (storefront checkout, bookings, a QR sign-up) cannot prove that the number you type is yours. For that reason we record only that you joined the programme; we never record marketing consent from a public form.

**By card online.** Where a business has switched on card payments, you pay through PayFast into **the business's own PayFast account**. You are taken to PayFast's secure page; Skhokho Labs never sees or stores your card details. PayFast's own terms apply to the payment page. Skhokho Labs does not hold your money and is not the merchant of record. **[ATTORNEY: confirm this description of the payment flow and whether any payment-intermediary obligations attach to Skhokho Labs.]**

A booking reserves a time with the business for the service you chose. The business may confirm, move or cancel it, and will contact you on the number you gave. Bookings are paid in person unless the storefront says otherwise. The business's cancellation terms are shown in its storefront footer. **[ATTORNEY: CPA section 17 (cancellation of advance bookings and reasonable cancellation charges) — confirm what the platform text must say and what each business may charge.]**

## 8. Delivery

A business may deliver with its own staff, or through Skhokholetha, or both. The storefront quotes the delivery fee before you order; for some addresses the fee depends on distance or on the delivery area you choose.

When your order is delivered through Skhokholetha:

- an independent driver, approved by Skhokho Labs, collects the order from the business and brings it to the address you gave;
- after the driver accepts the delivery, the driver sees your name, the delivery address, your delivery instructions and the contact number you gave for the order — nothing else;
- you receive your own delivery page link (on the confirmation screen and in your confirmation email if you gave one). That page shows the delivery status, the driver's position between collection and arrival, and your **drop-off code**;
- the driver may only mark the order delivered by entering the drop-off code you give them. Keep the code to yourself until you have your order in hand;
- the delivery page and any tracking link stop working 24 hours after the delivery ends.

The **Customer Delivery Notice** explains this in full and is shown before you choose Skhokholetha delivery.

Skhokho Labs runs the Skhokholetha network and is responsible for the way drivers are approved, dispatched and paid. **[ATTORNEY: liability for goods in transit on a Skhokholetha delivery — platform, driver or business — and the insurance position. Stated in section 11 once decided.]**
