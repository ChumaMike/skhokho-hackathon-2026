## 1. What the pilot is

1.1 Skhokho Labs gives the Merchant access to the Skhokho platform — storefront, catalogue, checkout, bookings, rewards card, order emails and the business console — to trade with real customers during the **pilot period**.

1.2 The pilot period runs from **[FILL IN: start date]** to **[FILL IN: end date]**, unless ended earlier under clause 9. It may be extended by written agreement (email is enough).

1.3 The pilot is **free**. No subscription, commission or transaction fee is charged by Skhokho Labs during the pilot. **[ATTORNEY: whether a nominal consideration is needed for enforceability.]**

## 3. What Skhokho Labs does

3.1 Keeps the platform running with reasonable skill and care, and tells the Merchant promptly about any outage or incident that affects it.

3.2 Provides a named support contact: **[FILL IN: name, WhatsApp/phone, email]**, available **[FILL IN: hours, e.g. Mon–Sat 08:00–20:00]**, and responds to a problem that stops trading within **[FILL IN: 2] hours** during those hours.

3.3 Handles the Merchant's customers' personal information **only** to provide the platform, as **operator** for the Merchant under POPIA, with the security measures in the Privacy Notice, and tells the Merchant without delay of any security compromise affecting that information. Skhokho Labs does not use the Merchant's customer data for its own purposes and never shares it with another business.

3.4 Does not change the Merchant's prices, catalogue or customer records except at the Merchant's request or to fix a defect, with the Merchant told.

3.5 Keeps a daily pilot log and shares findings that concern the Merchant.

## 4. What the Merchant does

4.1 Completes the onboarding checklist, including the **Legal** section of Settings (legal name, registration number if any, returns and cancellation terms, complaints contact), so the storefront shows the information ECTA section 43 requires, before publishing.

4.2 Is the **supplier** of everything sold: keeps prices, availability and hours accurate; fulfils orders; handles refunds and complaints promptly; complies with the Consumer Protection Act and with the health, safety and licensing rules of its trade.

4.3 Is the **responsible party** under POPIA for its own customers' information, uses it only for its relationship with those customers, obtains marketing consent only in person and records it truthfully, and passes any customer data request to Skhokho Labs within 2 business days.

4.4 Uses the platform honestly: no test orders presented as real sales, no sharing of staff logins, no attempt to see other businesses' data, no unlawful goods.

4.5 Gives feedback when asked and takes part in **[FILL IN: a weekly 20-minute call]** during the pilot.

## 5. Payments

5.1 During stage C customers pay the Merchant directly, in person. Skhokho Labs handles no money.

5.2 If stage D is switched on, customers pay by card into the Merchant's own PayFast account under the Merchant's own PayFast agreement. Skhokho Labs never holds funds and is not a party to those payments. A R5 verification payment is made to the Merchant's account when it is connected.

## 6. Personal information and confidentiality

6.1 The roles in 3.3 and 4.3 apply. The draft Privacy Notice and Retention Schedule (shared with the Merchant) describe what is stored, where (including that the database is hosted in the United States) and for how long.

6.2 Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential during and after the pilot. The Merchant may not disclose platform details, pricing plans or defects to third parties; Skhokho Labs may not disclose the Merchant's sales figures except in aggregated, anonymised form. **[ATTORNEY: standard confidentiality clause.]**

## 7. Liability

7.1 The Merchant is responsible for its goods, services, staff, own drivers, refunds and customer handling. Skhokho Labs is responsible for the platform working as described and for handling personal information as agreed.

7.2 **[ATTORNEY: a pilot-appropriate limitation of liability — for example, each party's liability to the other capped at R [FILL IN] for the pilot, excluding data-protection breaches, gross negligence and wilful misconduct; no liability for indirect loss; an indemnity from the Merchant for claims by its customers about its goods and services.]**

## 8. What happens after the pilot

8.1 At the end of the pilot the Merchant may continue on the published Merchant Terms (free until paid plans start, with at least 30 days' notice before any fee), or end its use under clause 9.

8.2 If the Merchant ends its use, Skhokho Labs provides an export of the Merchant's catalogue, orders, bookings and customer list at that business within 30 days, then deletes or de-identifies the Merchant's data under the Retention Schedule, keeping only what must be kept as a financial or legal record.

## 9. Ending early

Either party may end the pilot on **7 days'** written notice (email is enough), or immediately for a serious breach, unlawful activity or a safety concern. Orders already placed remain the Merchant's to fulfil and refund.
