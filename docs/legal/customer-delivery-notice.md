## Your delivery, in plain words

This shop uses **Skhokholetha**, the delivery network run by Skhokho Laboratory ("Skhokho Labs"). An independent driver, checked and approved by Skhokho Labs, collects your order from the shop and brings it to you.

**What the driver sees.** Once a driver accepts your delivery, they see your **name**, the **delivery address**, your **delivery instructions** and the **contact number** you gave for this order. They see this only for this delivery and only until it ends. They do not see your order history, your email or your rewards card.

**Your delivery page.** After you order, you get a link to your own delivery page — on the confirmation screen, and in your email if you gave one. Keep that link private: anyone with it can see your delivery and your drop-off code. On that page you can see:

- the status of your delivery;
- where the driver is, from the moment they collect your order until they arrive;
- your **drop-off code**.

**The drop-off code.** The driver can only mark your order as delivered by typing in the code you give them. **Give the code only when your order is in your hands.** Nobody from the shop or from Skhokho will ever ask you for it by phone or message. If a code is entered wrongly five times it stops working and the shop will send you a new one on your delivery page.

**If something goes wrong.** If the driver cannot reach you, the order goes back to the shop. Contact the shop (its number is in the storefront footer) to sort out a refund or a new delivery. If the shop does not help, contact Skhokho Labs at [FILL IN: support contact]. **[ATTORNEY: who is responsible for a lost, damaged or late Skhokholetha delivery and what the customer's remedy is — the answer must be stated here in one plain sentence.]**

**Your information.** Skhokho Labs keeps your delivery address and contact number for this delivery and the record of the sale, as set out in the Privacy Notice (/privacy). The delivery page and any tracking link stop working **24 hours** after the delivery ends.
