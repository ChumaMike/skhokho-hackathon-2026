## 2. What Skhokho provides

Skhokho is software for running a small business: a storefront (at a Skhokho address or your own domain), a product or service catalogue, a basket and checkout, bookings, a customer rewards card, in-house delivery tools, optional card payments through your own PayFast account, staff accounts with roles, order and booking emails, an assistant, and — where switched on — the Skhokholetha delivery network.

We may improve, change or retire features. Where a change removes something you rely on, we give reasonable notice in the console.

## 3. Your account, your storefront, going live

- You register with an email address and password and are the **owner** of the business on Skhokho. You may invite staff and give them roles; you are responsible for what your staff do in the console.
- Your storefront is invisible to customers until you **publish** it. Publishing is your decision; there is no approval queue. Before you publish you must complete the **Legal** section of Settings (section 6).
- You must keep your business details, catalogue, prices, hours and availability accurate. Customers rely on them.
- We may **suspend** a storefront or account for a breach of these terms, suspected fraud, a safety concern, or a lawful request. We tell you why, and orders already placed are still yours to fulfil and refund.

## 5. Payments from your customers

- **In person.** The default. You settle with the customer at collection or delivery; the console records the payment method.
- **Card online.** If you connect your own PayFast merchant account and verify it, customers can pay by card. Money goes **directly to your PayFast account**. Skhokho Labs never holds customer funds and is not the merchant of record. Your PayFast merchant agreement is your own; PayFast's rules on refunds and chargebacks apply to you.
- We store your PayFast merchant ID, key and passphrase encrypted, and use them only to send customers to your PayFast page and to verify PayFast's payment notices. We run a small verification payment (currently R5) when you connect, which PayFast settles to your account.
- Where a card payment arrives for an order that could not be completed, the console marks a **refund due**. You must refund the customer. **[ATTORNEY: whether Skhokho Labs carries any obligation here beyond notifying the merchant.]**

## 8. Skhokholetha deliveries

If you switch on Skhokholetha for an order:

- you request a driver for a **paid** delivery order; the console quotes the network cost before you confirm. The customer's delivery fee is applied to it; if the network cost is higher than what the customer paid, the console shows the **shortfall you will carry** and you confirm it knowingly;
- an approved independent driver collects the order using the **pickup code** shown in your console, and the customer confirms receipt with their own drop-off code. You never see the drop-off code;
- you may cancel before pickup. After pickup, a failed delivery is returned to you;
- the driver sees the customer's name, delivery address, instructions and delivery contact number, and your shop's name and address. Nothing else;
- Skhokho Labs approves, dispatches and pays drivers. **[ATTORNEY: responsibility for goods in transit, for a failed or late delivery, and for the customer's remedies — platform, driver or merchant. Insurance position.]**
