### Withdrawn from advertising

### 2.1 Feature keys other tabs will append

Per the workstream coordination rules, Tab 4 owns plans, limits and
`features.ts` packaging; other tabs may **append** a feature key with a comment
naming their tab, and must never reorder or rename one. Expected arrivals
include quote requests and extra storefront pages.

**Default policy for any newly appended key — this is the rule, not a
case-by-case decision:**

1. **A new key lands in NO plan.** It gets no `plan_entitlements` row until
   packaging is decided here. `resolveEntitlements` starts from
   `emptyFeatures()`, so an ungranted key is `{ enabled: false, limit: 0 }` and
   grants nothing anywhere.
2. **Therefore it is advertised nowhere.** `toShowcasePlan` derives the public
   feature list from the grant rows, so an unassigned key cannot appear on the
   pricing page by accident. This is the exact failure this migration had to
   undo for five keys (§2, "Withdrawn from advertising") and the default now
   prevents it.
3. **A key is granted only when it is both built and enforced.** The owning tab
   confirms there is a real enforcement site; Tab 4 then adds the grant row and
   updates the matrix in §2. A key with no enforcement site is not sellable,
   whatever it is called.
4. **Limit-type keys need a counter before they are granted.** A limit nobody
   counts is not a limit — see §3 and `modules/billing/limits.ts`, where
   `countFor` is the enforcement.
5. **The dormant-control exception:** a key meant as a ceiling rather than a
   gate (as `ai_token_budget_monthly` is today) must map "not granted" to *no
   cap*, never to zero, or it blocks everything the moment it is read. The
   pattern to copy is `allowanceFor()` in `src/lib/ai/anthropic.ts`.

Provisional intent for the two keys already flagged, to be confirmed when they
land: **quote requests** fits GROW (it is a lead-capture feature, which is what
GROW is sold on); **extra storefront pages** fits PRO alongside the custom
domain, since both are "this is my main business" signals. Neither is granted
until its tab confirms enforcement exists.

## 3. Limit enforcement

Enforced for `max_staff`, `max_products`, `max_services`.

**Policy — deliberately conservative:**

- **New creation is restricted** when a business is at or over its limit.
  A friendly, specific message naming the limit and the plan that lifts it.
  Never a crash, never a bare 403.
- **Existing rows are never deleted, hidden, archived or deactivated** because
  a plan changed. Not on downgrade, not at trial expiry, not after grace.
- **The over-limit state is surfaced plainly** on the plan page and wherever
  creation is blocked, so an owner is never confused about why.
- A business may sit over its limit indefinitely. It simply cannot add more.

Anything stronger than "restrict new creation" would need an explicit,
deterministic, separately-approved policy. There is none, so there is no
hiding or deletion anywhere in this build.

> Why: a merchant's catalogue and staff records are *their* business data. A
> billing state change is not a licence to touch it. Hiding a shop's products
> because its card failed would damage a real trading business, and the
> recovery path (which products? in what order?) has no honest answer.

## 4. Trial

- **14 days of GROW** for a newly eligible business.
- **No card required** — Phase A cannot take a card at all.
- `BusinessSubscription` with `planId = GROW`, `status = TRIALING`,
  `currentPeriodEnd = activation + 14 days`.
- **At expiry with no paid subscription: FREE.** Immediately, with **no grace
  period** — grace exists for a *failed payment on a paid plan*, not for the
  end of a free trial.
- No data is deleted. Over-limit products/staff/services stay exactly as they
  are (§3); the business simply cannot add more until it upgrades.

## 5. Billing anniversary

**The date the merchant activates their paid subscription.** Activated
17 November → renews 17 December → 17 January.

No forcing onto the first of the month, and therefore no first-month
proration.

Month-length edge case: an anniversary on the 29th, 30th or 31st clamps to the
last day of a shorter month (31 January → 28 February), then returns to the
original day-of-month afterwards. The anniversary day is kept, not drifted.

## 6. Grace

- `SUBSCRIPTION_GRACE_DAYS`: **7 → 10**.
- **Paid functionality remains fully available throughout grace.** Already
  implemented and tested: `subscriptionAccess` returns state `GRACE` with
  `usesSubscribedPlan: true`.
- Future card retry schedule (Phase B, documented now, **not built**):
  **day 0, day 2, day 5, day 8**, with grace expiring day 10 — two days of
  margin so the plan never lapses while a retry could still succeed.
- **Phase A performs no card retries**, because Phase A holds no card.

## 7. Dunning

Friendly, practical, specific. Amount, reference, bank details, the date
something changes, and an invitation to reply. No threats, no legalese. These
are businesses with thin month-end cash flow.

### EFT reminder cadence — 7-day terms

| when | message |
|---|---|
| due − 3 days | "Your invoice is due on <date>" |
| due date | "Due today" |
| due + 3 | reminder |
| due + 7 | reminder, stating the date access changes |
| due + 10 | final grace warning / downgrade confirmation |

### After grace

- Subscription resolves to **FREE** (the default plan, existing tested path).
- Paid add-ons become **`SUSPENDED`** — never deleted.
- **Merchant orders, storefront and core trading data are never deleted.**
- **Customer-to-merchant payments remain completely untouched.** A merchant
  behind on their Skhokho Labs invoice keeps trading and keeps being paid by
  their own customers.

## 8. EFT

**EFT is a permanent, first-class platform payment method** — not a stop-gap
for the period before cards work. A meaningful share of South African small
businesses will pay an invoice by EFT and will never put a card on file.

Terms: **7 days.**

## 9. Billing email

- `BusinessSubscription.billingEmail` **defaults to the business owner's
  email** at subscription creation.
- The owner can change it.
- All platform invoices and reminders go to `billingEmail`, so billing is not
  permanently tied to the owner's login address (bookkeepers, partners,
  changed hands).
- If `billingEmail` is ever null, fall back to the owner's email rather than
  sending nothing.

## 10. Invoice numbering

Format: **`SKHL-INV-YYYY-NNNNNN`** — e.g. `SKHL-INV-2026-000001`.

- `YYYY` is the calendar year of issue.
- `NNNNNN` is zero-padded to six digits.
- **The sequence resets each calendar year.**
- Allocation is atomic at the database layer (a per-year counter row taken
  under a row lock inside the issuing transaction), so two concurrent issues
  can never collide or reuse a number.
- Numbers are allocated **on issue** (`DRAFT` → `OPEN`), never on draft
  creation, so a discarded draft does not burn a number and leave a gap.
- A number, once allocated, is never reused — a cancelled invoice is **voided**,
  not deleted, and keeps its number.

## 11. VAT — dual-mode, unresolved

**No VAT registration assumption is made anywhere in the code.**

Phase A is built to support either mode, switched by configuration, and
**no real production invoice may be issued until the actual VAT status of
Skhokho Laboratory is confirmed.**

| | VAT registered | Not VAT registered |
|---|---|---|
| Document heading | "Tax Invoice" | "Invoice" |
| VAT number | printed | **absent** |
| VAT line | shown with rate | **absent** |
| `vat_cents` | computed | `0` |
| Advertised prices | **VAT-inclusive** (commercial preference) | the price is the price |

If registered, the intended commercial preference is **consumer/SMME-facing
VAT-inclusive advertised prices**: R 599.00 and R 1 299.00 are what a merchant
pays, with VAT derived at 15/115 of the total and shown as a line. This keeps
the advertised number honest for a non-VAT-registered spaza shop, which cannot
claim input VAT and only cares what leaves its account.

**No VAT registration number is invented.** Until a real one is configured, the
renderer refuses to produce a document labelled "Tax Invoice" — it cannot
accidentally issue a non-compliant tax invoice.

Rounding, if registered: VAT computed on the invoice **total**, not per line,
and rounded to the cent — one rounding, stated here so it is testable.

## 12. SaaS functionality vs Skhokho Labs managed services

A deliberate distinction, kept in language and in data.

**Content Studio is a software capability included in GROW and PRO.** The
merchant uses it themselves.

**Managed services are people doing the work for the merchant.** Sold by
enquiry, delivered by the studio (`ChumaMike/skhokho-labs`), and recorded as
`business_addons`.

| managed service | setup | monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Custom Website Service | R 1 500 | R 300 |
| WhatsApp Ordering Service | R 3 000 | R 1 000 |
| AI Voice Agent | R 2 500 | R 1 500 |
| **Managed Content & Marketing Service** | R 5 000 | R 1 500 |
| Speed-to-Lead | R 4 500 | R 1 000 |

The R 5 000 + R 1 500/month offering is renamed from "Content dashboard" to
**Managed Content & Marketing Service**, so it is never confused with the
Content Studio included in GROW.

**None of these become self-service purchasable in Phase A.** Enquiry →
Skhokho Labs closes the deal → an admin records the add-on → its monthly
portion then bills automatically alongside the plan.

**These are services, not merchant sales commissions.** Nothing here is a cut
of a merchant's turnover.

## 13. AI generation top-ups — deferred

**Not launched.** The architecture supports them (`ai_generations_monthly` is
metered per call in `src/lib/ai/anthropic.ts`, and `ai_token_budget_monthly` is
already wired as a dormant cost ceiling), but no top-up is sold until real
provider-cost and usage data comes out of the pilot.

Current allowances stand: **FREE 20 · GROW 150 · PRO 500** per calendar month.

Pilot exceptions are handled administratively with
`EntitlementOverride` (mandatory reason, optional expiry) — no code change, no
deploy.

## 14. Skhokholetha

**Delivery economics are not bundled into the software subscription.** Four
money streams stay distinct, with no shared table and no query that sums
across two:

| stream | records | direction |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant GMV | `Order`, `BusinessPaymentAttempt` | customer → merchant, never touches Skhokho Labs |
| Skhokho Labs SaaS + service revenue | `platform_invoices`, `platform_settlements` | merchant → Skhokho Labs |
| Skhokholetha settlement | `NetworkDelivery`, `OrderDelivery` | per delivery |
| Driver earnings | `DriverLedgerEntry`, `DriverPayout` | Skhokho Labs → driver |

## 15. Phase A — approved scope

Packaging documentation · limit enforcement (`max_staff`, `max_products`,
`max_services`) · `PAYFAST_MERCHANT_*` fail-closed refusal tests · additive
platform billing tables · invoice generation, lines and numbering · EFT
settlement recording · owner invoice history · Control Centre billing views ·
reminder and grace lifecycle · audit events · trial behaviour · plan activation
after a valid settlement.

Platform billing stays separate from the proven merchant PayFast path, and uses
the **`skhp_`** reference namespace. The customer-to-merchant implementation is
not modified except where strictly necessary for namespace
rejection/isolation tests.

## 16. Phase B — held

**No PayFast card tokenisation and no automated ad-hoc charging.** Held until
all five are true, none of them guessed:

1. The actual VAT status of Skhokho Laboratory is confirmed.
2. SaaS / merchant billing terms are legally ready (Tab 2 writes these;
   `docs/legal-dependencies.md` records "paid plans stay off" until they exist).
3. PayFast's token / ad-hoc **amount unit** is verified (cents vs rand — a 100×
   billing error if wrong).
4. PayFast's **API signing behaviour** is verified from an authoritative source
   or a controlled low-value live test. It is not safe to assume it matches
   either signature construction already in `src/lib/payfast.ts`, which differ
   from each other.
5. The **card-update behaviour that cannot be exercised in sandbox**
   (`createCardUpdateLink` returns "Onsite is not available in Sandbox mode")
   has a planned, controlled live rehearsal.
