# Incident drills

What each person sees and what the operator does, for the failures most
likely during the first pilot. "Rehearsed" means the behaviour is covered
by an automated test on the test database (named in the last column);
"documented" means the response is defined here and should be walked
through once on a call before stage C. Runbook numbers refer to
`docs/runbooks.md`.

| # | Incident | Customer sees | Merchant sees | Control Centre shows | Operator response | Safe recovery | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchant (owner) cannot log in | nothing — storefront unaffected | "email or password incorrect", or the account-recovery link; after 10 failed tries in 15 min, "try again later" | Security › `rate_limit_triggered` on `loginAccount` / `loginIp` for that hash; Users › the owner row (active? email confirmed?) | MERCHANT SUPPORT: confirm it is the owner by phone; have them use "Forgot password" (email) — if email is down, R4; never change a password from the console (no such command) | password reset by the owner; if the account is deactivated, PLATFORM OWNER decides | documented; login rate limits rehearsed in `rate-limit.db.test.ts` |
| 2 | Staff cannot access orders | nothing | "Your role doesn't have permission" or no Orders link | Security › `staff_permission_denied` rows; business page › Staff shows the person's preset and state (invited / removed) | SUPPORT asks the owner to check the preset (Counter/Manager see orders; Kitchen sees the kitchen screen only) or re-send the invite | owner fixes the role or re-invites; no platform action | rehearsed (`pilot-rehearsal.db.test.ts` permission matrix; `pilot-operations.db.test.ts` removal) |
| 3 | Storefront unavailable | error page or nothing loads | dashboard may also be down | uptime probe red (R2); if only one shop: business page status SUSPENDED or PENDING | R2: Vercel status → instant rollback; if one shop only: check status, publish state, domain status | rollback or reactivate; probe green; a test order places | documented |
| 4 | Payment succeeds but order not updated | paid, order still "awaiting payment" | order PENDING payment | Payments › attempt SUBMITTED with a provider notification that was rejected or `payment_notification_processing_failed`; Orders › needs attention "unpaid online" after 2 h | R3: read the notification outcome; a rejected notification (merchant mismatch / signature) means the merchant changed credentials — have them re-save; a processing failure means PayFast will retry | PayFast retries settle idempotently; if the merchant is sure the money arrived, they record it in person (PAID / method) and FINANCE reconciles against their PayFast statement | rehearsed (notify route tests, `merchant-operations.db.test.ts`) |
| 5 | Duplicate PayFast callback | nothing | nothing | Payments › attempt › second notification with outcome DUPLICATE_NOTIFICATION; "duplicates absorbed" counter | none required; note it if it repeats across merchants | nothing to recover — settlement happens once | rehearsed (`route.test.ts` notify duplicates; rehearsal "late duplicate cannot un-pay") |
| 6 | Customer paid the wrong / a duplicate amount | charged twice, or charged a different amount | notification PAYMENT_REFUND_DUE with the order; Buddy briefing mentions it | Payments › attempt REFUND_DUE with the reason (second payment / amount mismatch); Overview "Refund due" | FINANCE: confirm with the merchant; the merchant refunds in their own PayFast account; log the case | refund made by the merchant; the attempt stays REFUND_DUE (closing it is a P1 command) — record in the support log | rehearsed (second-payment and amount-mismatch tests) |
| 7 | Merchant pauses the store accidentally | "Orders paused — back at HH:MM" badge; checkout refused | Pause banner on Orders and Settings › Opening hours | business page › Trading: Paused until | SUPPORT tells them where Resume is (same place); pauses are capped at 24 h | merchant resumes; nothing else | rehearsed (`pilot-rehearsal` pause/resume, `pilot-operations` paused readiness) |
| 8 | Delivery search has no driver | "finding a driver" on tracking; no ETA | delivery SEARCHING on Deliveries; shop can cancel the request and assign an own driver | Overview `dispatch_down` (searching > 15 min, 0 online) or `dispatch_latency`; Deliveries › searching | R6: DRIVER OPERATIONS phones on-call drivers; tells the shop to use its own driver (BOTH falls back) or to cancel the request before pickup | request cancelled (reason) or accepted; order continues | rehearsed (no-driver refusals; searching views) |
| 9 | Driver cancels / fails after acceptance | status returns to "finding a driver" or shows failed | shop notification; delivery back in SEARCHING (pre-pickup cancel) or FAILED → RETURNING (after pickup) | Deliveries › delivery timeline: `cancelled` / `failed` with reason; incidents on the business page | DRIVER OPERATIONS: pre-pickup → nothing (re-dispatch runs); post-pickup → confirm the return with the shop's code, open a dispute if goods are missing, suspend the driver if it repeats | return confirmed; dispute decided; earnings adjusted by ledger entry if needed | rehearsed (state machine tests; cancel-with-open-delivery rehearsal) |
| 10 | Customer cannot access tracking | "link expired / not found" | nothing | Security › `rate_limit_triggered` on `trackingIp` if hammering; otherwise nothing | SUPPORT: the link is in the customer's email; the shop can read the status from Deliveries; never share the customer key or codes by phone | customer uses the emailed link; status given verbally by the shop | documented; tracking auth rehearsed in `skhokholetha-auth.http.ts` |
| 11 | Email fails | no confirmation / ready / on-the-way emails | notification `order_email_failed` on the order; everything else works | Health › email failures (24h); alert `email_failures`; Overview Email | R4: Resend status, key, domain; nothing is queued — the shop phones the customer for ready/dispatch | provider recovers; failures stop | rehearsed (provider failure leaves order state intact; `email_failed` events) |
| 12 | Daily cron fails | nothing | nothing immediately; unconfirmed payments stay pending longer | Health › job runs failed; alert `job_failures` / `cron_stale` | R5: read the error; run the task by hand (`manual.*`); check `CRON_SECRET` | next run ok | rehearsed (`runJob` failure test) |
| 13 | Admin account compromised / suspected | nothing | nothing | Security › failed logins / MFA spikes; Audit › unexpected privileged actions; alert `admin_auth_attack` | R7: phone every admin; Settings › Admin team › Reset MFA (reason); role change ends their sessions (epoch); rotate `JWT_SECRET` if a session may be stolen | re-enrolment; audit reviewed; no further failures 24 h | rehearsed (MFA, epoch, reset tests; `pilot-operations` revocation) |
| 14 | Database unavailable | error pages; checkout fails | dashboard errors | uptime readiness 503; alert `db_down`; `payment_notification_processing_failed` events once it is back | R1: Neon status / limits / last deploy; announce after 30 min | `/api/health/ready` 200; Payments › pending drains as PayFast retries | documented (not simulated — unsafe to fake) |
| 15 | Google Maps unavailable | distance-priced delivery addresses are refused as "couldn't be located"; zone and flat pricing unaffected; tracking map blank, text status fine | Skhokholetha requests refused for un-geocoded orders; own drivers fine | nothing specific (no alert); support cases mention "address not found" | TECHNICAL: Google Cloud status / key restrictions / quota; SUPPORT tells zone-priced shops nothing changes | provider recovers; retry the quote | rehearsed (un-geocoded order: own driver ok, network refused) |

Before stage C, walk through rows 1, 3, 4, 7 and 11 on a call with the slot
owners; before stage D add 4–6; before stage E add 8–10 and 15.
